Cricket stadium

Team match booking

Stadium booking is for team matches. A logged-in captain can select their own team and choose another listed team as the opponent.

  • Login is required before sending a stadium booking request.
  • Opponent teams are loaded from the team directory.
  • Booking status is tracked from the dashboard.
